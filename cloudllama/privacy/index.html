Cloud Llama

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Overview

Cloud Llama is a Chrome extension operated by Shed Design Labs. It connects to SoundCloud at your request, loads your liked tracks, and plays supported tracks in a retro music-player interface.

This policy applies to the Cloud Llama extension, the Cloud Llama pages on shedlabs.studio, and the supporting authentication and playback service.

Information Cloud Llama handles

When you connect SoundCloud, Cloud Llama may process:

  • Your SoundCloud username, user identifier, profile link, and basic profile information.
  • Metadata for tracks you have liked, including track and creator names, duration, availability, artwork or links, and stream information supplied by SoundCloud.
  • SoundCloud OAuth access and refresh tokens, encrypted or otherwise protected and used only to provide the connection you requested.
  • An opaque Cloud Llama session identifier and local preferences such as volume and player settings.
  • Limited technical information needed to diagnose failures, such as request status codes and service errors. Cloud Llama does not intentionally log OAuth tokens or signed playback URLs.

Information Cloud Llama does not collect

Cloud Llama does not read your general browsing history, the contents of unrelated webpages, passwords, personal messages, contacts, financial information, or precise location. It does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate your SoundCloud connection, retrieve your liked tracks, resolve supported playback streams, remember player settings, secure the service, and troubleshoot errors.

Storage and retention

Player preferences and an opaque session identifier may be stored in Chrome’s extension storage on your device. SoundCloud tokens are processed by Cloud Llama’s supporting service and are not placed in the distributed extension code.

Cloud Llama sessions are designed to expire within 30 days of creation. Short-lived authentication state and playback tickets expire sooner. Local preferences remain until you disconnect, clear extension data, or uninstall the extension. Operational records may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for security and troubleshooting.

Sharing and service providers

Cloud Llama communicates with SoundCloud to provide the features you request. Infrastructure providers may process limited data solely to host and secure the supporting service. We do not sell data or share it with data brokers, advertisers, or unrelated third parties.

SoundCloud is an independent service. Its handling of information is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

Security

Cloud Llama uses encrypted network connections for production service traffic, keeps the SoundCloud Client Secret out of the extension package, restricts access to the supporting service, and limits stored data to what is needed for the player. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion

You may disconnect SoundCloud from inside Cloud Llama to end the local session. You may also remove locally stored settings by uninstalling the extension or clearing its data. To request deletion of information retained by the supporting service, contact Shed Design Labs through the contact page.

Children

Cloud Llama is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Cloud Llama changes. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact Shed Design Labs through our contact page.
